# Decision tables: candidate applications for the JRSS-B resubmission

Prepared 18 Sep 2026. Five deep-dive scans (public data actually downloaded and profiled where noted). Scores are 1–5. "Natural" = case (a), the scrambling occurred without us; "Steward" = case (b), we apply the release mechanism to linked data we hold. Working files from the scans are in the session scratchpad; primary URLs are in the companion `Literature_scan_real_world_instances.md`.

Model reminder: Y = Π_X X β + Π_S W + ε within known blocks. Sub-cases: Π_X = I (only Y–location link lost), Π_S = I (only Y–X link lost), Π_X = Π_S (X is a function of location).

## Section D — Add Health verdict (from the public codebooks and Conditions of Use)

What is released: pseudo codes for state, county, tract and block group (12-character pseudo-FIPS, nesting preserved; "NOTE: These are pseudo codes and are not linkable to outside data sources"); Census region is the only real geography; no block level; Wave I `Spatial.xpt` gives within-community relative X/Y in metres. Wave I: 20,745 respondents in 37 states, 267 counties, 2,449 tracts, 4,411 block groups (≈8.5 respondents per tract, ≈9 tracts per county, but 80 communities so most counties hold one or two tracts and a few hold hundreds; Wave III: 5,938 tracts for 15,197 people ≈ 2.6 per tract).

Three facts decide it:
1. **The released tract covariates are exact public Census/ACS quantities** (tract population `tsf00001` values like 23,541; proportions to 14 decimals; county populations like 9,519,350 for LA), so within a county the pseudo-tract → true-tract map is trivially recoverable from X. Π_S is not unknown; it is a disclosure defect. You cannot use these X as covariates and treat the permutation as unknown.
2. **The Conditions of Use forbid exactly the analysis that would make the paper interesting**: "No attempt will be made to identify specific individuals, families, households, schools, institutions, or geographic locations not provided by Add Health" and "Restricted-Use Contracts do not permit the linkage of Add Health data with external data sets." A permutation-recovery demonstration, or attaching public tract exposures at true tracts, breaches both. Add Health's AI policy also forbids using LLM tools on individual-level data.
3. What remains legal is "β is robust to unknown Π_S with an exchangeable (non-spatial) W over pseudo-tracts", which discards the spatial GP that is the paper's point, and the two-level extension is worse (county/state covariates identify units outright).

Verdict: Add Health is a foil for the Introduction ("agencies protect location by relabelling blocks and pre-computing X at the true address"), not an application. Fallback value: low.

## Section E — What the tables imply for the model

Every real instance in Tables A–C has (i) block-specific permutations, (ii) unequal block sizes, and (iii) one of the three sub-cases rather than two independent permutations. The revision's generalisation is therefore data-driven, not a concession to Referee 2, and the paper should present the general model as the umbrella with the sub-cases as the applied objects. The common-permutation model survives only as the privacy-weakest bound (and as the phase-identification curiosity).

## Section F — Recommendation for the decision

- **Case (b) is the paper's application.** Primary: **Cook County ME** (real, current, identifiable deaths at exact addresses; unequal tracts; exposure surfaces; all four sub-cases runnable; compare against aggregation, within-tract donut jitter, DHS jitter, suppression on β̂ error and on per-record re-identification rate). Secondary/reproducibility: **Lawrence 1911–13** with Broen et al.'s published harness. Meuse becomes unnecessary or a one-figure illustration.
- **Case (a) as the "this happens naturally" demonstration:** **Broman/Attie1** (real block-specific off-by-one permutations on plates with full ground truth; REPAIR recovers documented mix-ups). If a 2-D natural instance is wanted, **Slide-seq unmatched beads** is the only one with strong spatial dependence and exact truth, at higher cost.
- **Motivation paragraph sources:** PMA/DHS household GPS never released; ONS 7–10% swap rate and the "(100−x)%" sentence; INSEE within-l+1 containment with Moran's I utility; Zhou–Dominici–Louis record-matching risk; Slawski & Sen "permutation recovery as linkage attack" (we run the arrow the other way); r-local unlabeled sensing as structural prior art (equal blocks, no dependence, no privacy).
- **Drop:** Add Health as application; Lougheed, forest-ALS, brain-atlas trio as data (keep as one-line motivations); the "Lin & Kolaczyk 2024" citation until verified.

## Section H — Comparators and the shape of the claims (added after discussion, 18 Sep 2026)

### Case (b): what "better than current mechanisms" must mean
- Comparison set (what a responsible steward would do instead): tract aggregation (ArealGP), within-block jitter (`maskmypy.donut(container=tract)`; DHS 0–2/0–5 km), suppression of location (with X pre-computed at the true address, UK Biobank style). Never "release everything".
- Frame: risk–utility map with ONE risk metric across mechanisms, as in Zhou, Dominici & Louis (AOAS 2010): expected fraction of records an intruder re-links correctly vs MSE of β̂. Theorems 1–2 give the analytic location of the permutation mechanism on the map.
- Utility claim (provable in the linear GP model): within-block permutation preserves every block-level statistic exactly (multisets of Y, X, s; block means; within-block variances; the pairwise distance distribution). Consequences: vs aggregation, keeps within-block variance of X (n obs instead of B); vs jitter, no measurement error in X read at a displaced point (attenuation does not vanish with n — Warren et al. 2016; Massoda Tonye et al. 2024 show covariate selection flips), while Theorem 2 gives consistency without a correction model; even block-mean-on-block-mean regression is unbiased under permutation and biased under jitter; vs suppression, keeps W estimable and keeps X when X is a surface.
- Caveat that must be stated: DHS's own covariates are resampled to 5 km, constant within cluster — no gain. The gain is for exposures varying within block (distance to road/water, 30 m NDVI, 100 m population, elevation). Simulations and Cook County must use such exposures.
- Risk claim: a priori k-anonymity = block size exactly (no density modelling; cf. Richter's verified neighbour). A posteriori the correlation structure leaks (Theorem 1). Adversary per mechanism: reverse geocoding for jitter (Cassa 2008), model-based re-linking (REPAIR as attacker) for permutation, 1/N for aggregation. The mechanism wins at matched risk only where β is estimable and per-block re-linking is not → requires block-specific keys; the Cook County R-U map should show common-key leaking and independent-key not.

### Case (a): is it solved, and comparators
- Broman/Attie1: the linkage was solved by R/lineup using hundreds of strong cis-eQTL genes jointly (predict genotype from expression per locus, count mismatches, nearest-neighbour assignment) — information our setting never has. The unsolved version is one gene / one cis-marker / plate as block with moderate LOD (10–30), asking whether the allelic effect is estimable when 33–55% of a plate's labels are wrong and the permutation is not recoverable from that pair. Comparators: naive (ignore mix-ups; attenuated), lineup-corrected oracle, Slawski & Ben-David sparse-permutation estimator, Slawski–West mixture model (CRAN `pldamixture`, `postlink`) — both assume small mismatch fraction and no blocks, so plates 1630/1632 are where they should fail and the block model should not. Limitation to state: W is a 1-D cohort effect along the well order, so this tests the permutation half, not the spatial-dependence half.
- Slide-seq unmatched beads: field's solution is to discard. Comparators: discard (naive); novoSpaRc/Tangram placement (optimal transport under smooth-expression assumption, no model, no β) followed by GP regression; REPAIR. Ground truth by artificially un-matching matched beads. The only natural case that tests the dependence half; cost = raw-image processing.
- Slawski-group and Pananjady-type estimators cover Π_S = I with iid errors; nothing in the literature handles a permuted GP (Π_S ≠ I) — that remains the novelty; nearest is Ota & Imaizumi 2026 (sparsely permuted, temporally dependent, induced).
