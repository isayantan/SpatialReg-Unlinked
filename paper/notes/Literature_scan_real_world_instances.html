Doubly-Unlinked Regression for Dependent Data — JRSS-B resubmission notes. Literature and data scan, prepared 18 Sep 2026; IPUMS correction 21 Sep 2026.

Real-world instances of the doubly-unlinked model — literature and data scan

Prepared 18 Sep 2026 for the JRSS-B resubmission of "Doubly-Unlinked Regression for Dependent Data" (Burman, Choudhury, Dey). Four parallel scans (statistical disclosure control; biology; epidemiology/exposure science; everything else). Every item below was checked against a fetched source; URLs are given so nothing here has to be taken on trust. Items the scans could not find are listed at the end so we do not overclaim.

Notation: Y outcome, X covariate, s location/time index, block = the known group inside which pairing is lost. Π_X breaks the Y–X link, Π_S breaks the Y–s link.

1. The headline finding

No agency mechanism, no released dataset and no naturally occurring measurement process independently permutes Y, X and s within a block. Every real instance is one of three special cases of the paper's model:

Family What is permuted Model Where it occurs
Geography swapping record keeps (Y, X); its small-area code / location is exchanged within a larger area Π_X = I, Π_S ≠ I US Census 1990–2010, ONS Census 2021, Eurostat 2021 (targeted record swapping); IPUMS-I (across districts, so not block-contained); location-swapping geomasks; Slide-seq unmatched beads; sediment cores; forest plots
Exposure re-read at masked location s is masked and X(s) is a surface evaluated at the masked point Π_X = Π_S DHS/LSMS/PMA/MICS GPS displacement with covariate rasters; all geomasking of point health data; Medicare / All of Us / Texas PUDF ZIP-level outcomes + 1 km PM2.5
Attribute shuffling / block-confined linkage error one attribute (Y or X) permuted among records within a stratum; location intact Π_S = I, Π_X ≠ I Dalenius–Reiss swapping, Muralidhar–Sarathy data shuffling, NCES PUFs; Chambers' exchangeable-linkage-error model and Salvati et al. (JRSS-B 2021) "linkage errors within blocks only"; within-plate sample mix-ups in eQTL studies (Westra 2011, Broman 2015)

The honest and, I think, strong way to pitch the general model is therefore as the umbrella that nests the three mechanisms that actually occur, plus the observation that the fully doubly-unlinked case is exactly what a steward gets by composing two existing mechanisms — geography swapping and attribute shuffling — within the same blocks. Correction (21 Sep 2026, after re-reading the source): IPUMS-International does not shuffle attributes. Its confidentiality page (https://international.ipums.org/international/confidentiality.shtml) lists four measures: "swapping an undisclosed fraction of records from one administrative district to another"; "randomizing the placement of households within districts to disguise the order in which individuals were enumerated" (this is record order, not attribute values); aggregating sensitive codes; and top/bottom-coding. So no agency is known to apply both geography swapping and attribute shuffling to one product. The general model is therefore to be proposed as the composition of two mechanisms that are each operational on their own (attribute swapping: Dalenius–Reiss 1982, Muralidhar–Sarathy 2006; geography swapping: US Census 1990–2010, ONS 2021, Eurostat 2021). The earlier sentence claiming IPUMS-I does both was wrong and has been removed from separation_theorems.tex.

Two things every real instance has and the current paper does not: block-specific permutations and unequal block sizes. Nothing found uses the same permutation in every block; the only real "common permutation" cases are hardware/pipeline bugs (a wrong channel map applied to every recording; time-interleaved ADC ordering) with no citable dataset. The common-key model should therefore be presented as the analyst-favourable / privacy-weakest bound, not as the applied case.

2. Candidates for case (a): a naturally occurring instance we could analyse

Ranked by (fit to the block model with block-specific permutations and unequal sizes) × (public data with ground truth) × (spatial/temporal dependence is real).

2.1 Documented within-plate sample mix-ups in expression genetics — Π_X ≠ I, real permutations, ground truth recoverable

Westra et al. 2011 (MixupMapper) found on average 3% and up to 23% of samples mis-assigned across five public expression–genotype datasets; in their own liver data "inspection of the RNA plate layout indicated that mix-ups had been introduced by pipetting mistakes" (columns swapped, rows inverted). Broman et al. 2015 (G3) found 18% mislabelled genotypes in a >500-mouse F2 cross; "plate positions of the DNA samples indicated a number of off-by-one and off-by-two errors". Block = 96-well plate; permutations are local and plate-specific; partial plates give unequal sizes. Y = expression of a cis-eQTL gene, X = genotype at the cis-SNP, s = well position (plate-position effects supply W, though weakly). Data: Attie1 at Mouse Phenome Database https://phenome.jax.org/projects/Attie1 ; R packages lineup/lineup2; GEO GSE11582 etc. Plate maps not in the public release (contact authors). Fit: permutation structure 4/5; spatial dependence 2/5; as evidence that "this happens" 5/5. Refs: https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/27/15/2104/400933 ; https://academic.oup.com/g3journal/article/5/10/2177/6028939

2.2 Bioturbated deep-sea sediment cores: single-foraminifera ages vs isotopes — Π_X = I, temporal domain, unequal blocks, ground truth on a subset

Lougheed et al. 2018 (Climate of the Past) measured 185 single shells from core T86-10P across 47 one-cm depth intervals (1–10 shells each): 100 isotopes-only, 49 ¹⁴C-only, 36 both. Bioturbation mixes shells of different ages within an interval, so within a depth block the multiset of ages (s) and the multiset of δ¹⁸O values (Y) are observed but unlinked; the 36 dual-measured shells are ground truth. GP over calendar age is the natural W. Block = depth interval; sizes 1–10. Caveat: ages and isotopes are on different shells (a multiset model rather than a literal permutation of the same units). Public: Table S1 at https://cp.copernicus.org/articles/14/515/2018/. Fit: 4/5 with caveat; the best temporal example and the cleanest "seriation" story.

2.3 Forest inventory plots vs airborne laser scanning — matching field trees to ALS crowns within a plot

National forest inventories record trees (DBH, species) on plots; ALS detects crowns (height, crown size); plot-centre GNSS error under canopy (up to ~20 m) and mis-detections make the within-plot correspondence unknown. Block = plot, 5–30+ trees, plot-specific permutation. Y = ALS attribute, X = field attribute, s = position; spatially correlated site/growth effects give W. Public: lidaRtRee vignette (15 plots, Jura) https://lidar.pages-forge.inrae.fr/lidaRtRee/articles/coregistration.html ; Monnet & Mermin 2014 https://www.mdpi.com/1999-4907/5/9/2307 ; Lamprecht et al. 2017 https://www.mdpi.com/2072-4292/9/5/505. Fit: 4/5 structure (closest to Π_S = I with X permuted, or Π_X = Π_S if s is taken from the field side); spatial audience-friendly.

2.4 Whole-mouse-brain atlases: region-dissected scRNA-seq + region-dissected snATAC-seq + CCF-registered MERFISH

The one setting where Y (expression), X (accessibility) and s (MERFISH coordinates) are all observed as region-level multisets with both links broken and strongly unequal region sizes (Yao 2023, Zu 2023, Zhang 2023, Nature). Fully public (Allen Brain Cell Atlas, https://alleninstitute.github.io/abc_atlas_access/intro.html). Caveat: the three multisets come from different cells, so it is a multiset/population model, not a permutation of the same n units; heavy to analyse. Fit 3/5; as an Introduction paragraph 5/5.

2.5 Slide-seq(V2) unmatched bead barcodes — Π_X = I, block = puck

Beads whose in-situ decoded position cannot be matched to their Illumina barcode are discarded by the standard pipeline; Optocoder reports naive match rates of 20–35% (up to ~62–70% with correction). Within a puck: expression profiles with lost positions + decoded positions with no expression. Public processed data (SCP354, SCP815) contain only matched beads; raw pucks needed. Fit 4/5 structurally; data effort high.

2.6 Others worth one sentence each

Drosophila 2-h collection windows with separate sci-RNA/sci-ATAC (Calderon 2022; temporal, block = window); Slide-tags unassigned nuclei; Neuropixels unit tracking across days (UnitMatch); camera-trap partial identity (Augustine 2018 AOAS); trajectory recovery from aggregated mobility data (Xu et al. WWW 2017 — bipartite matching recovers 73–91% of trajectories; the attack is permutation recovery driven by temporal correlation of W, a perfect disclosure-risk illustration; open code + GeoLife/Porto: https://github.com/ndsi6382/Trajectory_Recovery); SwapMob (Salas et al. 2018, deliberate within-cell identity swapping of trajectories, T-Drive); r-local unlabeled sensing (Abbasi–Tasissa–Aeron: block-diagonal permutation with a different permutation per block, equal block sizes — our unequal-block extension is a genuine generalisation; https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.06382); graph matching with known communities (Onaran, Garg & Erkip 2016: block = community); Ota & Imaizumi 2026 (only shuffled-regression paper with a day-blocked local permutation on real temporally dependent data, but induced: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14872).

3. Candidates for case (b): a release mechanism where we act as steward

3.1 Household surveys that hold household GPS but release displaced cluster centroids (DHS, PMA, MICS, LSMS-ISA)

DHS: "The DHS project never releases the GPS coordinates of individual households"; clusters displaced 0–2 km urban / 0–5 km rural / 1% rural 0–10 km, constrained to admin-2 (https://dhsprogram.com/pubs/pdf/SAR7/SAR7.pdf). PMA collects GPS for all listed households and releases only displaced EA centroids (https://www.pmadata.org/sites/default/files/2019-08/ENG_PMA_GPS_displacement_Protocol_20180115_.pdf). DHS geospatial covariates (malaria PfPR, rainfall, night lights, PM2.5…) are "extracted at the displaced cluster coordinates" (https://spatialdata.dhsprogram.com/references/DHS_Covariates_Extract_Data_Description_3.pdf). Researchers have obtained true coordinates through collaboration: Warren et al. 2015/16 (Uganda; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4946438/), Michler et al. 2022 JDE (LSMS-ISA; https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304387822000839), Massoda Tonye et al. 2024 (Cameroon MIS: "jittering modified our interpretation of the relationship between environmental predictors and malaria transmission"). Jittering-aware models exist (Altay, Fuglstad, Wakefield et al., GeoAdjust: https://journal.r-project.org/articles/RJ-2024-027/). Why it is the best case-(b) candidate: the steward already holds household points, already attaches exposure surfaces, and currently collapses households to one displaced centroid (aggregation + noise). Releasing within-cluster-shuffled household points instead is exactly our Π_X = Π_S model with block = cluster (20–30 households, unequal), and the utility/risk comparison against displacement is a live policy question. Ground truth requires an ICF/JHU-PMA/World Bank collaboration.

3.2 Address-based geomasks: "location swapping" and "verified neighbour"

Zhang, Freundschuh, Lenzer & Zandbergen 2017 (CaGIS 44:22–34, doi:10.1080/15230406.2015.1095655): replace a case location by another real residential location with similar characteristics within a neighbourhood; MaskMyPy implements it (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12065331). Richter 2018 (JESEE, doi:10.1038/jes.2017.17) "verified neighbour": surrogate from the k nearest verified residential parcels, optionally constrained to similar environmental-variable values — i.e., designed to control the Π_X = Π_S distortion of exposure. UN ISWGHS 2021 guidance lists location swapping as an option for survey GPS (https://unstats.un.org/unsd/statcom/52nd-session/documents/BG-3l-Spatial_Anonymization-E.pdf). Steffen, Körner & Drechsler 2025 (Statistics Surveys) review all masks (https://projecteuclid.org/journals/statistics-surveys/volume-19/issue-none/An-overview-of-data-protection-strategies-for-individual-level-geocoded/10.1214/25-SS151.pdf). Nuance: these draw the surrogate from all nearby addresses, not from the sample's own locations; a within-block permutation of the sample's locations — our model — has not been proposed in that literature. That gap is ours to fill.

3.3 Operational record swapping by statistical agencies (the Π_X = I anchor)

US Census 1990–2010: households swapped with a matched partner "across tracts but within the same county" (McKenna 2018/2019; https://www.census.gov/content/dam/Census/library/working-papers/2018/adrm/Disclosure%20Avoidance%20Techniques%20for%20the%201970-2010%20Censuses.pdf). ONS Census 2021: 7–10% of households swapped "within local authority districts"; fewer than 1 in 100 swaps crossed districts (https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/populationandmigration/populationestimates/methodologies/protectingpersonaldataincensus2021results). Eurostat 2021 targeted record swapping, implemented in sdcMicro::recordSwap (http://sdctools.github.io/sdcMicro/articles/recordSwapping.html). Attacks that re-link swapped records are permutation recovery (Abowd et al. 2025 HDSR; Christ, Radway & Bellovin 2022 IEEE S&P: "minority identification rates as high as 12%"). Classical refs: Dalenius & Reiss 1982 JSPI; Fienberg & McIntyre 2004; Shlomo & Skinner 2010 AOAS (swapping as misclassification of geography); Gomatam, Karr & Sanil (NISS TR 140); Duncan & Stokes 2004 R-U confidentiality map; Duncan & Pearson 1991 matrix masking (a permutation matrix is literally a row-operator mask); Domingo-Ferrer & Muralidhar 2016 "permutation paradigm": any anonymisation = attribute-wise permutation + noise (https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.04186).

3.4 US health microdata with coarse geography + public residence sets + public exposure surfaces (Introduction hook for mental health)

All of Us Controlled Tier releases only 3-digit ZIP; a 2023 Cancer Causes & Control paper complains this precludes fine exposure assignment; Burrows et al. 2025 (Environ. Res.) do PM2.5 vs incident depression/anxiety at ZIP. Medicare (Di et al. NEJM 2017, PM2.5 by ZIP of residence). Texas hospital discharge PUDF is free with ZIP/county. Residence sets are public (USDOT National Address Database; FEMA/ORNL USA Structures); 1 km PM2.5 surfaces are public. So the analyst knows the set of residences in a ZIP and the outcomes in the ZIP, but not the pairing: our model with Π_X = Π_S, unequal blocks, block-specific. Caveat: the population set of residences is known but which subset belongs to the cases is not (a subset-selection layer), except for near-complete enumerations (Medicare 65+, all births).

3.5 Public, reproducible ecological-inference dataset that is the binary-outcome special case

Woodward, Mork, Wu, Hou, Braun & Dominici 2023 (PLOS Glob. Public Health): county COVID-19 death counts + ACS PUMS individual covariates + tract-level PM2.5; code https://github.com/NSAPH/PM_COVID_ecoinference. For binary Y the within-block multiset is just the count, so this is block-unlinked regression with Π_X = Π_S and no steward needed (no individual ground truth exists, so it is an application, not a validation). Related: Wakefield & Shaddick 2006 Biostatistics; Jackson, Best & Richardson 2008 JRSS-A (ecoreg).

3.6 Digital phenotyping (the original motivation)

Beiwe-based NHS3/GUTS substudy: 2,394 participants, 11 TB of GPS; participants spend ~14.6 h/day at home so residence is directly recoverable; authors intend linkage "to spatial data" (Yi et al. 2024 JMIR PHS, doi:10.2196/55170). No GPS is released. UK Biobank pre-computes exposures at the true address and releases coordinates truncated to 1 km (Π_X = Π_S = I: a foil). Add Health pseudo-FIPS: block identity hidden, within-block pairing preserved — the opposite of our unlinking; useful only as a contrast ("agencies protect s by relabelling blocks or pre-computing X(s); within-block unlinking is the alternative whose utility for β we quantify").

4. Things to pre-empt (referee ammunition found by the scans)

  • Lin & Kolaczyk 2024 (NBER w32940) explicitly argue linkage error is not a privacy mechanism because "the randomness introduced by linkage errors is not independent of the data" (https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w32940/w32940.pdf). Our framing must be "disclosure-risk assessment in the SDC tradition (R-U map)", never a formal privacy guarantee.
  • The DP "shuffle model" (Bittau et al. 2017; Cheu et al. 2019) permutes whole locally-randomised messages across all users to hide identity; it does not unlink attributes within a record and is a false friend. Cite only to disambiguate.
  • Location swapping is not a bijection among sampled records; do not describe it as our model, describe ours as its permutation analogue.
  • Slawski & Sen (JMLR 2024) already frame permutation recovery as a linkage attack ("Y_n may represent anonymized sensitive data while an adversary holds auxiliary data X_n"); cite as precedent for "recovering Π is the disclosure risk".
  • Salvati, Fabrizi, Ranalli & Chambers (JRSS-B 2021), same journal: "linkage errors are possible within blocks but not across blocks" with area = block — the block model is an accepted assumption in the linkage literature; cite prominently.

5. Not found (do not claim)

  • Any dataset or mechanism that independently permutes Y, X and s within a block.
  • Any public dataset produced by location swapping or verified-neighbour masking.
  • Any application of shuffled/unlinked regression to real spatial or temporal data (only Meuse, and Ota & Imaizumi's induced day-blocked permutation on air-quality data).
  • Any citable dataset with the same permutation in every block (only anecdotal wiring/channel-map bugs).
  • Statistics Canada / ABS / Statistics Netherlands swapping practice; SOEP-Geo / Understanding Society coordinate handling (search budget exhausted).
  • Full text of Zhang et al. 2017 and Richter 2018 (paywalled; descriptions rely on reviews and abstracts).
